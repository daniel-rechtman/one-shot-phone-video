# Worked script — "פתיחת סתימה" (drain unclog)

The script that produced the hook version on 2026-09-22. Copy the shape, not the content.
15 seconds, 9:16, MiniMax H3 at 2K, one character sheet as `Image 1`, $1.96.

---

FORMAT: Vertical 9:16 smartphone video. One continuous handheld take, no cuts. Amateur
operator - the frame drifts and re-centers, and the operator physically steps closer
instead of zooming. No music. 15 seconds.

CHARACTER (locked to Image 1): the chubby toddler girl from Image 1, identical face, same
two pigtails with pink hair ties.
WARDROBE: tiny navy plumber's overalls streaked with grey, worn over her white strawberry
t-shirt. Oversized yellow rubber gloves swallowing her arms to the elbow. Barefoot.
LOCATION: cramped bathroom, wet tiled floor, open sink cabinet, an LED work light lying on
the floor, a black bucket, tools scattered.
LIGHT: harsh phone flash inside the cabinet, hard shadows, everything outside it dim.

**0:00-0:01.5 HOOK**
SHOT: Extreme close-up, phone held a hand's width from the sink rim, tilted down. The basin
fills the entire frame.
ACTION: Filthy black water brims over the rim and sheets down the cabinet door onto the
floor. A small yellow-gloved hand punches down into the black water up to the elbow and
gropes for the drain.
SOUND: water sheeting onto tile.

**0:01.5-0:05 REVEAL**
SHOT: The operator whips the phone down and steps back. Low angle from floor level looking
into the open cabinet.
ACTION: The toddler is already on her back on the wet floor, head and shoulders inside the
cabinet. She grips the chrome P-trap with a pipe wrench almost as long as she is, braces
both bare feet against the cabinet frame and heaves. The nut squeaks a quarter turn. She
resets her grip and heaves again. Deadpan, never acknowledging the camera.
SOUND: metal squeak, her effort grunt, dripping.

**0:05-0:09 THE BREAK**
SHOT: The operator crouches and pushes in too close. The frame shakes, loses focus for a
moment, recovers.
ACTION: The trap breaks free. A slug of grey water dumps into the black bucket and splashes
across her cheek. She does not flinch. She sets the wrench down on the tile.
SOUND: heavy slop of water into a plastic bucket, wrench clinking on tile.

**0:09-0:12.5 THE PULL**
SHOT: Tight on the open mouth of the pipe, her gloved hand entering frame from the left.
ACTION: Two gloved fingers go into the pipe. She draws out a rope of matted black hair and
grey sludge, hand over hand, far longer than should fit. It keeps coming. She glances up at
the lens, one eyebrow raised, then drops the whole mass into the bucket.
SOUND: a wet drag out of the pipe, then a heavy wet slap in the bucket.

**0:12.5-0:15 PAYOFF**
SHOT: The operator stands and tilts up to the basin - the reverse of the opening frame.
ACTION: Two fast wrench turns spin the trap back on. Her gloved hand reaches up and flips
the tap. Clean water rushes straight down the open drain, free and loud. She gives one
satisfied nod at the lens.
SOUND: the trap tightening, the tap, a clean unobstructed rush of water down the pipe.

CONTINUITY: her face and pigtails identical to Image 1 in every beat; the yellow gloves stay
on throughout; the black bucket stays in the lower left of frame from 0:05 onward.

---

## Why each part is there

| Beat | Job | The craft move |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | stop the scroll | opens on the worst moment, character not yet visible |
| REVEAL | the joke | the toddler is already mid-struggle, never introduced |
| THE BREAK | raise stakes | water hits her face and she does not flinch |
| THE PULL | the money shot | longest beat, most words, the one glance at camera |
| PAYOFF | close the promise | reverse of the opening frame, clean water |

The five-beat arc — **hook, reveal, escalation, money shot, payoff** — ports to any trend
format. Swap the content, keep the skeleton and the timings.
